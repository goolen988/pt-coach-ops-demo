Jack — your PT AI starter kit

A practical first trial with Bart. Version 0.1 · 26 September 2026

The aim is simple: make the weekly check of your clients and diary less fiddly, using the AI app you choose. You do not need another subscription to a new product from us. Any ChatGPT/Claude plan or usage costs remain with that provider.

Three things to try

Review my clients. See which records deserve a check, with the reason and source. Help me write a follow-up. Get an editable, supportive draft after you verify the facts. Help plan my week. Spot overlaps and tight gaps in a supplied calendar, then suggest admin time.

This release does not predict who will quit, change training/nutrition plans, send messages, change bookings or run in the background. It does not yet connect to your coaching system or computer. You stay in control.

Start here

  1. Open START_HERE.html in a browser, or continue with this page.
  2. Choose one route: setup/CHATGPT.md or setup/CLAUDE.md. Use your own account, not Bart's client-data workspace. Do not buy a plan just for the demo; check what is available first.
  3. Start with demo/pt-demo.json. Every client is fictional. Follow the prompt in PROMPTS.md and compare with demo/EXPECTED_RESULTS.md.
  4. Complete YOUR_COACH_PROFILE.md with Bart before using real data. For the first session, a verified small sample is enough; no bulk export of private histories is needed.
  5. Open a fresh chat in your configured workspace and run the same job yourself. That is the main onboarding test.

What to bring to the session

Your laptop and usual AI account; the name of the coaching/calendar tools you actually use; and one weekly admin task you would like to make easier. Do not email client records or passwords. We can use the demo if permissions or exports are not ready.

What you should leave with

One configured route, one result you can trace back to its source, one repeatable next-run instruction, and a clear list of what still needs work. The three workflows share a setup; we prioritise the one you find most useful.

Two important limits

The kit can only analyse records you supply or an explicitly authorised supported source. It does not know what happened outside them. Each new review needs current data and the up-to-date contact log; an old conversation is not a reliable operational database.

No fee, subscription, ongoing support entitlement, testimonial or promotion commitment is created by receiving this pack. Any paid pilot or continued support is agreed separately. You are welcome to explain the demo to colleagues without sharing real client information.

Your first session with Bart

Suggested appointment format: 45 minutes. This is a session agenda, not a promise that your app/account has already been configured.

Part What we do What you should see
0–5 min Pick the weekly job and check your actual app/tool setup One concrete job, not “learn all AI”
5–12 min Configure one app route with the synthetic kit Skill or Project instructions actually loaded
12–20 min You watch one source-backed review and request a draft Facts separated from missing data; no message sent
20–25 min Check diary conflicts and a candidate admin block Proposed only, not a changed calendar
25–35 min Complete the privacy/input gate; map a small authorised real sample if ready A real result only when source/permissions allow it
35–42 min You start a fresh chat and run the chosen task without Bart prompting A repeatable workflow, or a clearly recorded blocker
42–45 min Check the evidence, agree a next self-run and record feedback Clear outcome and no hidden support/promotion obligation

If real data is not ready, we still finish the installation and synthetic self-run. We record demo onboarding complete / real-data validation pending, not a failed relationship and not a validated real deployment.

The self-run check

Can you open the right workspace, choose the current file, ask the reusable request, identify a source reference, explain why missing data is not a missed workout, and tell which actions have not happened? Try it in a new conversation, not only by continuing Bart's demo.

What we measure

Record preparation/upload time, time to useful result, corrections, the amount of help needed and whether the answer changed what you actually did. Include data preparation in the time: a fast answer with 30 minutes of manual exporting is not automatically a saving.

After the session

Try the same job at its next natural occurrence. Save the result in your approved workspace and log actual sent messages. Tell Bart the specific step that helped or failed, without sending private client data. No reminder or support automation has been created by this pack.

Optional peer introduction

After you have tried it, a helpful next step is an introduction to one colleague with the same problem. You do not need to endorse it, share your client data or promote a result you have not observed.

Before using real client information

Use the synthetic demo until the coach/organisation approves the real-data path. The first trial needs only pseudonymous client IDs, agreed dates, attendance/check-in statuses, contact permissions/history and minimal calendar blocks. It does not need nutrition, injuries, health histories, body measurements, photos, message inboxes or passwords.

Keep the original records and identifying lookup under the coach's control. Do not send them to Bart or add them to the distributed Skill ZIP. Changing names to IDs is pseudonymisation, not a guarantee of anonymity. Do not publish the workspace or a chat containing private data.

The OAIC explains that a gym collecting health information can be a health service provider covered by the Privacy Act even when small. Whether and how your particular data/workflow may be processed needs the responsible organisation's assessment. This kit is not a compliance opinion. [S5]

Check your actual provider/account controls. OpenAI describes model-training controls for personal accounts and different business defaults. Anthropic distinguishes consumer settings from its commercial defaults. Disabling training is not the same as zero retention, local processing, automatic authorisation or guaranteed Australian data residency. [S4, S6, S7]

The bundled checker is offline and has no network calls. Uploading its inputs to an AI app is still cloud processing by that provider. Local execution of the checker is an alternative for the numerical review; moving its resulting client-level report into an AI app also needs approval.

Every first real run should record who approved the use, the specific account/workspace, input coverage, minimum fields, who can see it, where outputs will live and how temporary copies will be removed. If those answers are missing, continue with fictional data.

Do not include real client details in feedback buttons, screen recordings, case studies or community demonstrations. Any case study or testimonial needs a separate decision and appropriate permission.
